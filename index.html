Where things stand — August 2026

Apply for the right intake. Timing is the part most applicants get wrong.

Canadian institutions run three intake cycles a year. Applying to the wrong one — or applying too late for study-permit processing to catch up — is one of the most common, avoidable mistakes Nigerian applicants make. Here's where each cycle stands right now.

Closed · In session

Fall 2026 (September start)

Applications largely closed Dec 2025 – Mar 2026

The largest intake, with the widest program choice and most scholarships. Classes are already underway. If you missed it, the Blueprint shows you how to plan for Fall 2027 with proper lead time instead.

Open now

Winter 2027 (January start)

Most application windows close Oct – Nov 2026

This is the realistic, active window for anyone starting their preparation today. Fewer programs than Fall, but real seats, real DLIs, and enough runway left — if you move now.

Opens soon

Summer & Fall 2027 (May / Sept start)

Applications typically open late 2026

Mainly college diploma and certificate programs for Summer; the full university selection returns for Fall 2027. Research now if you want maximum choice later.

IRCC is currently processing most study permit applications in 8–16 weeks, and Nigerian applicants should budget toward the longer end. Combined with school-level Winter 2027 deadlines landing in October–November, the next few weeks are the realistic window to submit a complete, well-organized application and still make a January 2027 start. Most institutions also still require a valid Provincial Attestation Letter (PAL) or Territorial Attestation Letter (TAL) alongside your Letter of Acceptance from a Designated Learning Institution (DLI).

These windows summarize general, typical patterns across Canadian institutions and are not official dates. Every school and program sets its own deadline — always confirm your specific intake, DLI status, and PAL/TAL requirement directly with your chosen institution and on the official IRCC website before applying.
